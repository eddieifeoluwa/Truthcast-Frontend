🌍 Why this matters
Embassies and consular officers do rough digital background checks on almost every applicant. The US requires 5 years of social media handles on the DS-160. The UK, Canada, and Schengen countries all cross-reference your online footprint against your application.
  • What we do: audit your application against what you actually look like online, catch contradictions, surface credibility risks.
  • How it helps: gives you a chance to fix real issues (update your LinkedIn, correct a namesake collision, publish a Passport) BEFORE the officer notices.
  • What we won't do: help you hide real facts, misrepresent your history, or deceive the officer.
◉ Ethical spine. This tool exists to help you present yourself accurately and clearly. If you're considering hiding a criminal record, a prior visa refusal, or misrepresenting your employment — please talk to a licensed immigration attorney instead. Getting caught in a misrepresentation is far worse than disclosing honestly.
