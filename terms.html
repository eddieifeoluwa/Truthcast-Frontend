2. What TruthCast does

TruthCast is a reputation intelligence platform for public figures, creators, and brands. Core capabilities:

  • Trust Score — an AI-computed credibility assessment based on your public content
  • Perception Mirror — how the internet talks about you (news, social, search)
  • Identity Shield — cross-platform impostor detection using face verification (AWS Rekognition), reverse image search (Google Lens via SerpAPI), and multi-signal fusion
  • Reputation Graph — historical tracking of your credibility signals over time
  • Advisor — AI-powered recommendations for content, growth, and reputation moves

We're a software service, not a lawyer, a takedown agent, or a PR firm. We surface information; you decide what to do with it.
