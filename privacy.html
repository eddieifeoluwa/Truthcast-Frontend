3. Biometric data (special category)

Important: Face photos count as biometric data under UK DPA / GDPR (Article 9). We handle them with the special safeguards required by law.

When you use Identity Shield's face verification:

  • Your reference photos are stored as raw bytes on our secure Postgres database in the EU/UK — never on third-party image hosts
  • They are transmitted only to AWS Rekognition for face comparison. Rekognition performs the comparison and returns a similarity score; per AWS's terms, Rekognition does not retain the images after the comparison completes
  • We use them exclusively to detect impostor accounts using your face — never for marketing, training our own models, or sharing with anyone else
  • You can delete any reference photo any time from Settings → Identity Shield → Reference Photos. Deletion removes it from our database and prevents future comparisons

Legal basis for biometric processing: your explicit consent, given by uploading a reference photo and using the Identity Shield feature. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the photos and disabling the feature.

4. Why we collect it

Every piece of data has a purpose:

  • Account & connected platforms — to log you in, associate scan results with you, and know what to monitor
  • Public content about you — to compute your Trust Score, generate perception analysis, and detect impersonation
  • Reference photos — to face-verify candidate impostor accounts
  • Payment — to charge for paid tiers and manage subscriptions
  • Usage — to detect abuse, size infrastructure, and improve the product
  • Communications — to send you alerts you asked for (impostor detected, weekly brief) and answer support requests

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. We don't train AI models on your data for use in other customers' products.

5. Legal bases we rely on

Under UK DPA / GDPR we rely on:

  • Contract (Art 6(1)(b)) — to deliver the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests (Art 6(1)(f)) — for usage analytics, security, and abuse detection, always balanced against your rights
  • Consent (Art 6(1)(a) & Art 9(2)(a)) — for biometric data (face photos), optional WhatsApp alerts, and any marketing communications. Withdrawable any time
  • Legal obligation (Art 6(1)(c)) — for accounting, tax, and lawful investigator requests

8. International transfers

Some sub-processors are based outside the UK/EEA (primarily US). Where we transfer personal data internationally we rely on:

  • Adequacy decisions by the UK government where they exist (currently applies to the US via the UK-US Data Bridge)
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with each vendor for other transfers
  • Additional technical safeguards (encryption in transit + at rest)

10. Your rights

Under UK DPA / GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of everything we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct anything that's wrong
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — delete your data. Use Settings → Delete Account, or email us
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (we provide JSON export)
  • Restrict processing — pause certain uses of your data (e.g. stop weekly briefs)
  • Object — to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — for anything we do based on consent (biometrics, WhatsApp alerts)
  • Complain — to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe we've mishandled your data

To exercise any right, email hello@truthcast.io. We'll respond within 30 days and never charge for a legitimate request.

11. Security

We take security seriously because our product's value depends on it:

  • All connections are HTTPS/TLS encrypted
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plaintext)
  • API access is authenticated with signed JWTs
  • Database access is restricted to our application servers
  • Reference photos are stored as bytes in Postgres, never on public URLs
  • AWS access keys are stored only in Railway environment variables, never in source code
  • We run automated dependency scans and patch known vulnerabilities within 7 days

If we discover a data breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the ICO within 72 hours as required by law.

12. Cookies & tracking

TruthCast uses only essential cookies and local storage — no analytics or advertising trackers:

  • tc_token — your login session (JWT)
  • tc_user — cached user profile for fast dashboard load
  • tc_scan_data, tc_perception_data — cached scan results to avoid re-fetching
  • tc_currency — your preferred display currency
  • tc_ref_code, tc_pending_code — referral / founding code tracking

All of these are functional / strictly necessary — no consent banner is required under UK PECR because we don't use tracking cookies.

13. Children

TruthCast is not for children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email hello@truthcast.io and we'll delete it.
