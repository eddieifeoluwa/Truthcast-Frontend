Our story

Behind every name, there’s a story.

Stories built on generations of legacy. Names carried with pride. Brands built with sweat and sacrifice. TruthCast was started to make sure those stories are understood, and protected, in a world that moves too fast to listen.

Why TruthCast exists

Perception travels faster than truth.

Every public voice and every growing brand knows a version of the same story. Years of work, of showing up for their community, of building something real. And then, one day, a moment they didn’t see coming: a clip taken out of context, an account pretending to be them, a rumour that spread through group chats before they’d even heard it.

What hurt most wasn’t the moment itself. It was not knowing. Not knowing how far it had travelled, who believed it, or whether to respond at all.

We realised that the ones with the most to lose, the podcasters, journalists, creators, leaders and growing brands, had the least visibility into how they were actually seen. Big corporations have whole teams watching their reputation. Most voices and brands have nothing.

Yet in a world where perception travels faster than truth, reputations can be shaped by narratives beyond our control.

That’s why TruthCast exists. To give every voice a clear view of their impact, an early warning when their reputation is at risk, and the confidence to act with intention.

Our mission

Help people understand their impact and protect their reputation.

We turn the noise of the internet into a clear picture: how you’re perceived, where your voice lands, and what could put your name at risk.

Our vision

A world where every voice is seen clearly.

Where reputations are judged on the full story, not the loudest clip, and where the legacy you leave is the one you meant to build.
