Questions

Good questions.

Do I need to be famous to use TruthCast?

No. If people talk about you in public, whether that’s a local community, a niche audience or a national stage, your reputation matters. TruthCast works at every size.

Does TruthCast work for brands and businesses?

Yes. Brands use TruthCast to see how customers and communities talk about them, catch fake pages and misuse of their name early, and understand the creators and voices they partner with. The Brands & Teams plan is built for this.

I host a podcast. What does TruthCast do for me?

It shows how your show and episodes are being discussed, which moments are being clipped and shared, and whether anything is spreading out of context, so you can respond with the full episode when it matters.

Does TruthCast verify who I am or check my credentials?

No. TruthCast doesn’t verify identities or credentials. We help you understand how you’re perceived, measure your impact and protect your reputation.

Will TruthCast post or reply on my behalf?

Never. TruthCast gives you clarity and recommendations. Every decision about what to say, and when, stays with you.

Where does the information come from?

Public conversation: social platforms, news and other openly available sources connected to your name. We show you where each insight comes from.

Can my team or agency use it with me?

Yes. The Pro & Teams plan lets managers, agencies and PR teams support several people from one place.
