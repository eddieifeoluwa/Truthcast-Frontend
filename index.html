Responsible AI

Intelligence you can question. Evidence you can inspect.

TruthCast is not a black box. Every score comes with a source trail. Every verdict can be challenged. Every signal is attributed. This is not a design compromise — it is the point.

01
Explainability by design

Every Trust Score links directly to the signals that generated it. Users can drill into any dimension and see the evidence beneath the number.

02
No invented claims

TruthCast never fabricates credentials or certifications. If a signal is unavailable, we say so. Absence of evidence is stated, not silently excluded.

03
Right of response

Subjects can flag errors, add context, and submit evidence to update their profile. Trust intelligence should be a conversation, not a judgement.

04
Bias awareness

We actively monitor for systematic bias in our signals. Coverage gaps are disclosed. Cultural and regional differences are accounted for in scoring.
