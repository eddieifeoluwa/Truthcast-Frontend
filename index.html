Questions

Answered up front.

The five questions everyone asks. Straight answers.

How is the Trust Score calculated?
Four weighted signals: fact-check pass rate, cross-platform consistency, source diversity, and audience alignment. Each is defensible — click any score in the dashboard to see the exact claims, sources, and computations behind it. No black box.
Which platforms do you scan?
YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook — plus podcasts (RSS feeds), news mentions (15,000+ sources), and Reddit discussions. New platforms added quarterly.
Is my data private?
Your dashboard is private. Your Credibility Passport is public only if you choose to share it. We're a UK-registered company subject to UK-GDPR — data lives in EU (Ireland) region. You can request full export or deletion any time at support@truthcast.io.
Do brands or journalists have to have accounts?
No. Anyone can view a shared Credibility Passport without signing up — that's the point. If they want to vet multiple creators or run their own scans, they'd need an account (Brand tier).
What happens after the founding cohort fills?
Creator Pro moves to £27/month for new users. Founding members keep £9/month forever, regardless of price changes. That's the deal.
